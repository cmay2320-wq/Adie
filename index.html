      Open when…

      You're stressed out

      Put the pen down for a minute. Breathe in for four, out for six. The stress you feel is your brain trying to protect you, so thank it and tell it you're okay. You've prepared more than you realise, and all you need to do now is the next small thing.

      You can't focus

      That's normal and it doesn't mean you're failing. Set the focus timer for 25 minutes, put your phone in another room and pick one topic. If your head is too loud, walk for ten minutes first, then come back. Starting badly still counts as starting.

      You don't feel smart enough

      That voice is lying to you. Being nervous about exams is what happens when someone cares, and you care a lot. Think about everything you have learned this year. Nobody gets this far by accident.

      You just finished a paper

      Stop. Don't replay it and don't compare answers with anyone. It's done and you did your best. Eat something, rest your head, and get ready for the next one. I'm proud of you for walking in and finishing it.

      It's the night before an exam

      Close the books at a sensible hour. Pack your things, set two alarms and go to sleep. Nothing you read in the last hour is worth as much as a rested brain tomorrow. You know this already. I'll be cheering for you in the morning.
